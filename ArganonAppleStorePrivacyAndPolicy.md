# Arganon (አርጋኖን) for iPhone and iPad

## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 October 2026
**Last updated:** 20 October 2026
**Applies to:** the Arganon app distributed through the Apple App Store (iOS and iPadOS). The Android app distributed through Google Play has its own policy.

### Introduction

Arganon ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile application for browsing, streaming, downloading and studying Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo mezmur (hymns) and kidase (liturgy). It is developed and maintained by Mikias Tekalign (Melloss), reachable at mellossdev@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why it is collected, who it may be shared with, how long it is kept, and what choices you have.

Arganon has no user accounts. You do not register, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location in order to use the App. Most information stored for your personal use stays on your device.

### What we collect

#### 1. Information stored only on your device

Your favourites, downloaded hymns and liturgies, custom categories, chosen colour theme, category filters, lyric font size, playback preferences, and any publishers you have blocked or categories you have reported are saved in the App's own storage on your device. This information is not sent to us as part of the ordinary use of these features.

Hymns and liturgies you download for offline listening are stored in a part of the App's storage that is **excluded from iCloud and device backups**, so they do not use your iCloud storage. They remain until you delete them in the App or remove the App.

Deleting the App removes this information from your device.

#### 2. A random app identifier

The first time you open the App, it generates a random identifier (a UUID such as `f47ac10b-58cc-…`). It is not your device's hardware identifier, the Apple advertising identifier, your phone number, email address or name.

On iOS this identifier is also kept in your device's **Keychain**, so it stays the same if you delete and reinstall the App. That is what keeps an Arganon Premium subscription and the categories you published linked to your device after a reinstall. It is kept "this device only": it is not synced to iCloud Keychain and does not move to another iPhone through a backup.

The identifier is sent to our server:

- each time the App starts, together with the device information described in section 4, so that we can deliver notifications and count how many installations use which version of the App;
- when you publish, edit, vote on, report or delete a public category;
- when you send feedback or a mezmur request;
- when you buy or restore Arganon Premium (section 5).

#### 3. Content you choose to submit

Some features let you voluntarily send information to our server:

- **Mezmur requests**: the hymn title and artist name you type.
- **Feedback**: the text you write and the hymn it concerns, so that we can reply.
- **Public categories**: the title, description and hymn list of any category you choose to publish, together with the random app identifier. A published category is reviewed by us before it is visible to anyone else. Once approved it is visible to other users of the App.
- **Votes**: which shared category you voted on, and the random app identifier, so that one installation cannot vote repeatedly on the same category.
- **Reports**: when you report a public category, the reason you choose, any note you write, and the random app identifier. Reports are read only by us, to decide whether to remove the category.

Everything in this section is voluntary. If you do not use these features, nothing is submitted. Please do not put personal information about yourself or anyone else in category titles, descriptions, feedback, requests or reports.

Other users never see your random app identifier. Each published category carries a separate code, derived from it in a way that cannot be reversed. The App uses that code only so that a user who blocks a publisher no longer sees any of that publisher's categories.

#### 4. Device and notification information

When the App starts, it sends our server:

- the random app identifier;
- the platform (iOS), the App's version and your device's language;
- your device model (for example `iPhone15,2`) and iOS version;
- whether notifications are allowed for Arganon;
- a notification token.

The App uses **Firebase Cloud Messaging** ("FCM", provided by Google), which delivers through **Apple Push Notification service**, to tell you when new hymns are added and to deliver replies to your feedback and decisions about categories you published. The token identifies your installation for message delivery only. You can turn notifications off at any time in **Settings → Notifications → Arganon**.

We use this information to deliver notifications, to understand which App versions are still in use (for example, before requiring an update), and to keep the service working. We do not use it to identify you by name.

#### 5. Arganon Premium (in-app subscription)

Arganon Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription, sold through the App Store, that removes advertisements. It does not unlock any additional content: every hymn, download and liturgy is free for everyone.

**Payment is handled entirely by Apple.** We never see or receive your name, Apple ID, email address, card or bank details.

When you subscribe or restore a purchase, the App sends our server the signed transaction that Apple provides, together with the random app identifier. This lets our server confirm with Apple that the subscription is genuine and decide whether ads should be shown on your device. The transaction contains the product purchased, the purchase, renewal and expiry dates, the App Store transaction identifiers, the storefront country, and whether the purchase was a trial, a renewal or a refund. Apple also sends our server **App Store Server Notifications** when a subscription renews, expires, is refunded or changes.

We keep this information for as long as the subscription is active, and afterwards for as long as needed for accounting, refunds and dispute handling, and to meet legal obligations.

You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple account (**Settings → your name → Subscriptions**), or from **Settings → ማስታወቂያ አስወግድ** in the App. Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

#### 6. Advertising

Unless you subscribe to Arganon Premium, some features are unlocked by watching a short advertisement. Examples are downloading a hymn, downloading a whole category or a full liturgy, saving or publishing a category, and exporting an image or video without the Arganon mark.

Advertisements are requested and managed by **Appodeal**, an advertising mediation platform, and may be supplied by advertising partners connected through Appodeal. To serve, measure and protect advertisements, Appodeal and those partners may process:

- the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), **only if you allow tracking** (see below);
- IP address and network information;
- device model, iOS version and language;
- advertisement impressions, clicks and other ad interactions;
- diagnostic and performance information about the ads.

**App Tracking Transparency.** Before any advertisement is requested, iOS shows Apple's tracking prompt for Arganon. If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the advertising identifier is not available to Arganon or its advertising partners, and you will still see advertisements that are not personalised to you. Every feature works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

Where privacy law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent form is shown before advertising starts, and you can change your choice later in **Settings → የማስታወቂያ ፈቃድ** in the App.

Arganon does not send your religious affiliation or any similar sensitive information to advertising providers. We do not control the individual advertisements shown by third parties and do not endorse what they advertise.

If you subscribe to Arganon Premium, the advertising system is not started at all, and you are not shown the tracking prompt.

#### 7. Technical information from ordinary use

When the App fetches the hymn list, streams or downloads audio, or talks to our server, our hosting providers automatically receive the technical information every internet request carries: IP address, the date and time, and general device and network information. We use it to deliver content, operate and secure the service, diagnose problems and prevent abuse. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

### What we do not collect

Arganon does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, calendar, precise location, or anything else on your device that you have not chosen to share.

**Photos.** When you choose a background photo for an image or video you create in the ተግባር tools, iOS's photo picker gives the App only the photo you selected. When you save a creation, it is added to your Photos. Photos are never uploaded to us.

**Camera and microphone.** Arganon does not record audio or video. iOS lists these permissions because the photo picker component the App uses supports them.

Arganon contains no analytics SDK of its own. We do not sell your information.

### Permissions the App may ask for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Tell you about new hymns, replies to your feedback, and decisions about categories you published |
| Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) | Let advertising partners use the advertising identifier for more relevant ads; optional, and not asked if you have Arganon Premium |
| Photos (add only / selected photo) | Use a photo you pick as a background, and save the images and videos you create |
| Background audio | Keep a hymn or liturgy playing when the screen is off or you switch apps |

If you decline a permission, only the feature that needs it is affected.

### International data transfers

Our server is in Germany, and some providers process information in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it, the providers use contractual safeguards or other recognised transfer mechanisms.

### How long information is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete it in the App or delete the App. The Keychain copy of the random app identifier remains until you reset your device or erase it from the Keychain.
- **Device registration (section 4):** while the installation keeps contacting our server, and for a limited period after it stops.
- **Published categories:** while they remain published, unless you delete them or we remove them.
- **Votes:** for as long as the category they are on exists.
- **Reports:** for as long as needed to act on them, and afterwards as a record of moderation decisions.
- **Feedback and mezmur requests:** for as long as needed to respond and keep an appropriate support record.
- **Subscription records:** as described in section 5.
- **Server logs:** for as long as reasonably necessary for operation, security and abuse prevention.
- **Advertising data held by Appodeal and its partners:** according to their own policies.

### Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

In practice, for Arganon:

- **Categories you published:** delete them yourself in the App.
- **Anything else we hold:** email mellossdev@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, include your app identifier so that we can find your records: open **Settings → ስለ (About)** and **long-press the Arganon logo** to copy it, then paste it into your email.
- **Advertising:** change your tracking choice in iOS Settings, and your consent choice in the App, as described in section 6.
- **Subscription:** managed and cancelled in your Apple account.

We aim to answer privacy requests within 30 days.

### Children

Arganon is not directed at children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age where you live, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through feedback, a request, a report or a published category, contact us and we will delete it.

### Security

All communication between the App and our server uses HTTPS/TLS, and access to our server is restricted. Only the installation that published a category can change or delete it, apart from our moderation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold.

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above changes when we do, and where the law requires it we will also tell you in the App.
